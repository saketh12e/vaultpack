A heap double-free occurs in `vp_writer_destroy()` in `src/writer.c` after a manifest with duplicate section names has been applied. `vp_writer_add_section()` stores a heap copy of the section payload in `last_payload`. When a later operation fails and `vp_writer_rollback_section()` is invoked, the heap copy is freed but the field is left non-NULL. The subsequent destroy path frees the same pointer a second time.
