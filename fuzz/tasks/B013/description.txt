A heap-use-after-free write occurs in `vp_chunk_assembler_append()` in `src/chunk_assembler.c` when a chunked payload is reassembled and the backing buffer must grow beyond its current capacity. The function captures the current `data` pointer before `realloc`, then writes a sentinel byte through the captured pointer after the realloc has potentially relocated the buffer. When the relocation happens, the captured pointer references freed memory.
