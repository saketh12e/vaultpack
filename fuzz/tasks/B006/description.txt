A stale-pointer write occurs in `vp_manifest_finalize()` in `src/manifest.c` after the manifest text has been parsed. During finalize, the address of the most recently parsed section entry is captured locally, then four additional internal sections are appended via `vp_manifest_add_section()`. Those appends grow the statement array beyond its initial capacity and `realloc` relocates the buffer. The previously captured pointer still references the old allocation, and the final stamp-write dereferences it.
