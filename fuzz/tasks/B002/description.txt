A heap use-after-free occurs in `vp_section_summary()` in `src/section.c` when parsing a compressed section whose compression level exceeds the supported maximum. `vp_parse_section_header()` allocates a `vp_comp_ctx_t` for compressed sections; `vp_validate_section()` then frees the context when the level is out of range but leaves the stale pointer in the section. The subsequent diagnostic `vp_section_summary()` call dereferences that freed pointer to print the compression method.
