A heap use-after-free write occurs in `vp_index_populate()` in `src/index.c` when a sub-entry insert loop triggers `vp_index_grow()`, which calls `realloc()` and may relocate the slots buffer. A pointer to the first slot is captured before the populate loop and written through after the loop. When the buffer moves, that captured pointer is dangling and the post-loop write touches freed memory.
